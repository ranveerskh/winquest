Back-safe rules

Lower-left back pain is usually 1-2, but sometimes 7 after bending or standing after sitting. So the plan avoids heavy barbell deadlifts and ego squats for now.
  • Pain 0-2: continue with clean form.
  • Pain 3-4: reduce weight or range.
  • Pain 5+: stop that exercise.
  • No pain down leg right now - good sign, but don't ignore changes.

Exercises to treat carefully

  • Heavy deadlift: avoid for now.
  • Barbell squat: only light/controlled if pain-free; machines are safer for now.
  • Leg press: don't let lower back round.
  • Hip thrust: ribs down, don't over-arch.
  • Back extension: optional only if pain-free.

Bloating control

  • Avoid cola and fried food most of the week.
  • Keep dinner lighter than lunch.
  • Use jeera/ajwain/ginger in chana/rajma/soya meals.
  • Don't mix huge raw salad with late heavy dinner.
  • Walk 5-10 min after dinner when possible.
